# Shorts-to-long-form conversion: do Shorts drive subscribers and long-form views, and how to make them

Context: Lolcow Cinema, a new clip channel (Kino Casino, Channel 404 News) posting long segments, compilations, short clips and about 1 vertical Short per day (90-179 s). The owner believes posting many more Shorts will pull people to the channel and its long videos. Researched 2026-10-21. Labels used: [OFFICIAL] = YouTube's own statement or docs; [THIRD-PARTY] = vendor or analyst study; [ANECDOTE] = single creator or channel report; [OLDER] = before 2024.

## 1. What YouTube itself has said about Shorts helping long-form, audiences, and "cannibalization"

### Takeaway
YouTube's official line (May 2025) is that Shorts reach new viewers, "can help drive traffic" to long-form, and that it has "not seen evidence" of Shorts hurting long-form, because the two formats have separate recommendation systems. YouTube has published no conversion numbers to back this up. The one independent before/after study (an arXiv preprint, not peer reviewed) found long-form views dropped after large long-form channels started Shorts.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube Blog "Shorts truths: Debunking common myths about YouTube Shorts" (The YouTube Team, 6 May 2025). Myth 6, "Shorts hurt long-form performance": YouTube says it "built separate recommendation systems optimized for each" format and "we haven't seen evidence of Shorts negatively impacting long-form performance." — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)
- [OFFICIAL] Same post, Myth 2 ("Shorts don't drive long-term growth"): Shorts are "a powerful tool for reaching new and diverse viewers"; it describes Shorts viewers as "viewers who actively explore a wide range of topics and creators." Myth 4: "shorts can help drive traffic to your long-form content too!" The post gives no statistics. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/debunking-common-myths-about-youtube-shorts/)
- [OFFICIAL, reported] Trade coverage of that post (ahead of Brandcast 2025) adds that YouTube says its systems "understand cross-format viewing" but mainly optimize within each format; no named spokesperson, no figures. — [The Drop / dropmedia.co.uk, 11 May 2025](https://www.dropmedia.co.uk/?p=4473)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube Blog "YouTube Shorts deep dive: A conversation with product lead Todd Sherman and creator Jenny Hoyos" (28 Jan 2025), with a Creator Insider video. Key points: "99.9% of the views are coming from the Shorts feed" (so Shorts thumbnails matter little for discovery); hook in the first second; Shorts work as self-contained "bits"/mini-stories. It says nothing about Shorts driving subscribers or long-form viewing. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/); [Creator Insider video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_tWy-_otnUc)
- [OFFICIAL, docs, as summarized by a third party] YouTube Help's recommendations page says trying formats like Shorts won't by itself confuse the algorithm or hurt channel performance, because each video is judged on its own. YouTube uses "what a user discovers about topics or channels to inform recommendations across formats." — [YouTube Help 16559651](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559651), summarized in [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [OFFICIAL, docs] YouTube Studio's Audience tab has a "Formats your viewers watch on YouTube" report: it shows whether a channel's viewers watch videos, Shorts or live on YouTube overall in the last 28 days. That is the in-product way to see format overlap. — [YouTube Help 9314416](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416), cited by [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [OFFICIAL, OLDER/undated] YouTube's global head of music, Lyor Cohen, has said he is "deeply concerned" that some viewers might watch only short-form clips and never go on to long-form. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=168257)
- [OLDER, 2022] YouTube's early "multiformat creator" push claimed channels uploading both Shorts and long-form saw better overall watch time and subscriber growth than long-form-only channels, and that many new subscribers were Shorts viewers who would not otherwise engage with a long-form creator. Example given: Rosanna Pansino reportedly doubled main-channel viewership year over year after starting Shorts. — [Tubefilter, 15 Jun 2022](https://tubefilter.com/2022/06/15/youtube-shorts-multi-format-creator-ian-boggs-rosanna-pansino/)
- [THIRD-PARTY, contradicts the official line for some channels] arXiv preprint 2402.18208, "Shorts on the Rise: Assessing the Effects of YouTube Shorts on Long-Form Video Content" (Rajendran, Creusy, Garnes; Feb 2024; not peer reviewed). Sample: 250 creators over 100K subs, before/after each creator's first Short, no control group. It found a significant drop in long-form views: mean -743,589 views per channel (p=0.011), and -920,189 for long-form-focused creators (p=0.005). Creators already focused on short-form showed no significant drop. Limitation: it can't separate Shorts from platform-wide trends or reverse causality. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18208), summarized in [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [THIRD-PARTY] OverseerOS looked at about 37 channels and found no universal long-form penalty after channels started Shorts. But a stricter check gave weaker results, and in a small exploratory comparison, channels posting more Shorts had softer long-form performance. — [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form-views)
- [Source-quality warning] CreatiCalc reports that three widely repeated quotes about Shorts and long-form, attributed to Todd Sherman, Rene Ritchie and Todd Beaupré, could not be traced to any primary source. It says the only dated on-record statement is the May 2025 myths post. — [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)

### Inferences
- "No evidence of harm" is not the same as "Shorts drive long-form". YouTube says the formats are recommended separately, so a Shorts viewer becomes a long-form viewer mainly through topic/channel signals and explicit links, not automatically.
- For a clip channel, the realistic risk isn't cannibalization of existing long-form viewers. It's that many Shorts bring in "Shorts-only" subscribers who never watch long-form. That inflates the subscriber count without long-form watch time.

### Gaps
- I found no verifiable Rene Ritchie or Todd Sherman statement with numbers on Shorts-to-long-form conversion; repeated quotes appear untraceable (per CreatiCalc).
- YouTube has never published an aggregate Shorts-to-long-form conversion rate or a subscribers-per-view comparison.

## 2. The "Related video" link on Shorts

### Takeaway
Each Short can carry one persistent "Related video" link to a video on the same channel. It shows as a title-styled link under the Short's title, set in YouTube Studio on desktop. It's the only clickable path from a Short to a long video, since YouTube removed clickable links from Shorts descriptions and comments in 2023. There's no official click-through data. Third-party figures are thin.

### Cited Findings
- [OLDER, Aug-Sep 2023 launch] Creators can add a single "related video" link when editing a Short in YouTube Studio on desktop. It can point to any other video on the same channel: long-form, livestream or another Short. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-adds-shorts-links-limits-links-elsewhere/495463)
- [OLDER, 2023] On mobile, the link appears directly below the Short's title as the linked video's title with a small play icon (not a button). Setup at launch: Studio > Content > edit Short > "Related video". The vidIQ article says the channel must be verified, and the link can't be added from mobile. It frames the feature as partly replacing the clickable links YouTube removed from Shorts comments, descriptions and live chat. — [vidIQ, 8 Sep 2023](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos/)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube's creator blog has a step-by-step guide that describes the link as routing traffic to any long-form video, livestream or secondary Short on the channel. Suggested uses: Short to long-form, chaining a Short series, promoting a livestream. — [YouTube Blog, related videos traffic guide](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/)
- [OLDER] At launch, YouTube said click analytics for related-video links would come "in a future update"; none of the sources found confirm what reporting now exists. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-adds-shorts-links-limits-links-elsewhere/495463)
- [THIRD-PARTY] AIR Media-Tech (2026) recommends filling in the Related Video field on every Short and adding a spoken CTA. It treats raising Shorts-to-long-form conversion from 0.04% to 0.5% as "worth more than doubling your upload frequency." That benchmark has no stated methodology. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- [THIRD-PARTY, unverified] A creator-tools newsletter says Related Shorts can lift long-form views for new uploads by 10% or more. — [Spotter "The Spot"](https://spottercommunity.beehiiv.com/p/increase-views-from-related-shorts)
- [ANECDOTE, unverified] Strategy blogs cite a major entertainment channel getting a 20% lift in long-form views from systematic linking. One vendor also claims pinned comments with a full-video link convert at 1-3%, "significantly higher" than the related-video link. The 1-3% claim conflicts with the fact that comment links on Shorts are not clickable since 2023 (see SEJ above), so treat it as dubious. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-hurting-long-form-views)
- [THIRD-PARTY] vidIQ best practice: link a video that closely continues the Short's topic, and say/show a CTA pointing at the link ("tap the link below for the full ...") with a reason to click. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos/)

### Inferences
- The implied conversion order of magnitude is well under 1% of Shorts viewers per Short (AIR's 0.04% baseline vs 0.5% "good"). At 10,000 Shorts views, that is roughly 4-50 clicks to the long video. Volume of Shorts alone won't move long-form much unless the link is relevant and the Short sets up the payoff.
- For clip channels, the natural pattern is: Short = a self-contained moment from a segment, Related video = that exact segment or compilation, plus an on-screen/spoken "full segment linked below".

### Gaps
- No official or large-sample click-through rate for the Related video link was found. Whether Studio now reports related-video clicks (promised in 2023) is unconfirmed; check Studio > Shorts analytics.

## 3. Other Shorts-to-long-form levers

### Takeaway
Besides the Related video link, the levers with any support are: same-topic Shorts (topic overlap drives cross-format recommendations), cutting Shorts from your own long videos (YouTube's "Edit into a Short"/import links back to the source), verbal/on-screen CTAs, series chaining, and keeping Shorts a minority of uploads. Pinned comments and descriptions can't carry clickable links on Shorts.

### Cited Findings
- [OLDER, Jul 2022] "Edit into a Short": a channel owner can cut a section of their own long video into a Short in the mobile app (up to 60 s at launch). The published Short links back to the source video ("viewers can see the original video too"). Only the original uploader can import. Sources disagree on whether footage from other videos can be added. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2022/07/28/youtube-creators-can-now-turn-old-vods-into-new-shorts/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtubes-added-a-new-option-to-cut-your-long-form-videos-into-shorts/628416/); [Routenote](https://routenote.com/blog/turn-your-youtube-videos-into-shorts-in-seconds/)
- [OFFICIAL, docs] YouTube uses topic/channel discovery "to inform recommendations across formats", so Shorts on the same subjects as the long-form are the mechanism for organic crossover. — [YouTube Help 16559651](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559651) via [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [THIRD-PARTY] AIR's 18,000-channel study (Mar 2025-Mar 2026) found the best Shorts share is 25-40% of uploads (about 1-2 Shorts/week alongside 3-5 long videos). Above about 55% Shorts, it says Shorts "start crowding out the content audiences subscribed to, and long-form performance drops." Channels that pivoted abruptly (+30 points Shorts ratio, n=1,247) underperformed both steady groups. Example: Entertainment 100K-1M median 51,600 subs vs 72,400 (mix) / 73,600 (long-only). It recommends shifting gradually over 2-3 quarters. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- [THIRD-PARTY] Paddy Galloway's (older, about Apr 2023; 33 channels, 5,400 Shorts) explanation for big channels converting better from Shorts: they make better Shorts and use better calls to action inside them. — [The Publish Press](https://news.thepublishpress.com/p/are-shorts-worth-it)
- [OFFICIAL] Hook in the first second; Shorts as self-contained mini-stories (Hoyos/Sherman). — [YouTube Blog, Jan 2025](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/)
- [THIRD-PARTY] Suggested diagnostic: in Studio, check whether viewers who found the channel via Shorts go on to long-form, and keep Shorts on the long-form topic. Use a separate Shorts channel only if the audience is genuinely different. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-hurting-long-form-views); [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-questions/should-you-keep-shorts-and-long-form-videos-on-the-same-youtube-channel/)
- [THIRD-PARTY, inference by author] CreatiCalc argues that cutting highlights out of long-form can lower conversion: if the Short already delivers the best moment, there's less reason to click through. This is the author's opinion, not data. — [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)

### Inferences
- For Lolcow Cinema: Shorts should be "trailers" that leave something unresolved (setup and first beat of a bit) and Related-link to the full segment, rather than complete highlights. Kino/404 segments are naturally serial, so a "Part 1 of the bit" Short linking to the full segment fits.
- Channel page shelves, end screens and cards don't exist on Shorts in the feed. The Related video link and in-video CTA are the only in-Short levers.

### Gaps
- No data found on Shorts shelf placement on the channel page, series playlists for Shorts, or "end hook" effectiveness for long-form conversion specifically.
- Whether "Edit into a Short" now supports the 3-minute Short length was not confirmed in this session.

## 4. Creator data and case studies: subscribers per view, and whether Shorts subscribers watch long-form

### Takeaway
Per view, Shorts convert to subscribers somewhat worse than long-form (about 17 vs 23 subs per 10K views in one older study). The large 2025-26 dataset (AIR) shows that mixing in Shorts helps subscriber counts in some niches but correlates with fewer subscribers in entertainment and gaming at scale. Evidence that Shorts subscribers watch long-form is weak and mostly anecdotal; overlap is claimed to be low (~10%).

### Cited Findings
- [THIRD-PARTY, OLDER ~Apr 2023] Paddy Galloway study (33 channels, 5,400 Shorts): long-form converted 22.7 subscribers per 10,000 views, Shorts 16.9. The reverse held for channels over 1M subscribers. Original study not located; figures via secondary summary. — [The Publish Press](https://news.thepublishpress.com/p/are-shorts-worth-it)
- [THIRD-PARTY, 2026] AIR Media-Tech, 18,000 English channels, Mar 2025-Mar 2026, median subscribers; correlational, and AIR sells channel audits. Long+Short mix beats long-only by up to 41% in fitness (100K-1M: 120,000 vs 84,900), business (+39%), education (+37%) and food (+31%). Long-only leads in gaming (100K-1M: 70,100 vs 37,850), beauty and kids. **Entertainment** is roughly tied at small scale (100K-1M: 72,400 mix vs 73,600 long-only) and long-only leads at scale (1M-10M monthly views: 214,000 vs 183,000, +17%; 10M-50M: +90%). AIR's entertainment advice: "test freely at small scale; treat Shorts as secondary at scale." — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- [ANECDOTE via AIR] An 11,000-sub Canadian entertainment channel: Shorts views 169,724 to 615,683 in a quarter, and total watch time +624%. A kids' network went from 24M to 299M views in a quarter, with the Shorts share of traffic rising from 6.6% to 81.8% and revenue +386%. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- [THIRD-PARTY, Jul 2026] vidIQ "first 10 videos" study: Shorts reach thresholds more easily, but this measures views, not subscribers. 86.5% of channels had at least one of their first 10 Shorts reach 1,000 views within 30 days, vs 57.8% for long-form. Per video, 56.6% of Shorts vs 30.2% of long videos reached 1,000. At 10,000 views: 48.0% (Shorts) vs 30.8%. — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/)
- [THIRD-PARTY, unsourced] Strategy blogs claim same-channel Shorts and long-form audiences often overlap only about 10%, attributed to unnamed "YouTube strategists". They call Shorts-acquired subscribers who never watch other uploads "dead subscribers". — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-hurting-long-form-views); [Subscribr (new channels)](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-vs-long-form-new-channels)
- [OFFICIAL, docs] YouTube notes that a subscriber count "reflects the number of times people have subscribed, not necessarily the size of its current active audience." — via [CreatiCalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-long-form)
- [ANECDOTE] One Shorts-focused channel reported a single interview Short bringing in over 20,000 subscribers. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/youtube-shorts-for-long-form-growth-monetization)
- [THIRD-PARTY] The view metric changed on 31 Mar 2025: a Shorts "view" now counts every start or replay with no minimum watch time. The old metric is "engaged views" (Studio Advanced Mode), and revenue and YPP eligibility stay tied to engaged views. Subs-per-view ratios from before and after that date are not comparable. — [TechCrunch, 26 Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404562/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views.html)

### Inferences
- Lolcow Cinema sits in entertainment/commentary, where the large dataset shows no subscriber advantage from mixing in Shorts at small scale, and an advantage for long-only at larger scale. "Many more Shorts" isn't supported as a growth strategy by this evidence. Moderate volume with strong linking is.
- Using Galloway's ratio as a rough guide: 1M Shorts views would bring about 1,700 subscribers, but with low overlap (if ~10% is right) maybe a few hundred of them watch long-form.
- The channel's own "Formats your viewers watch" report and the traffic-source split for long videos (Shorts feed / "Related video" traffic) are the real test; check them before scaling volume.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 study with a large sample directly measures subscribers per 1,000 Shorts views against long-form; Galloway's is the only direct comparison found, and it predates the 2025 view-counting change.
- No data found specific to podcast or stream clip channels (the closest analogue to Lolcow Cinema).
- No measured data on what share of Shorts-acquired subscribers watch long-form.

## 5. Shorts monetization and YPP thresholds

### Takeaway
Shorts earn far less per view than long-form: creators keep 45% of their share of a pooled Shorts ad pot. Third-party 2026 data puts Shorts RPM at about $0.07-0.20 in most niches, 3-14% of long-form. YPP can be reached via 10M Shorts views in 90 days or 4,000 long-form watch hours in 12 months (both with 1,000 subs). Shorts-feed watch time doesn't count toward the 4,000 hours.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Shorts revenue: ads in the Shorts feed fund a Creator Pool per country. A creator's share is their portion of engaged views from monetizing creators' Shorts, and creators keep 45% of it, with or without music. Licensed-music Shorts first divert part of the pool to music partners, based on the number of tracks. YouTube's worked example: 1M engaged views = 1% of the pool = $900 allocated, $405 paid. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- [OFFICIAL] YPP eligibility: 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public long-form watch hours in the last 12 months, or 10M valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Watch hours from Shorts-feed views don't count toward the 4,000 hours. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- [THIRD-PARTY] Revenue and YPP eligibility are based on engaged views, not the post-Mar-2025 "views" count. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted)
- [THIRD-PARTY, 2026] AIR Media-Tech, 274 channels, 13 niches, 3,044 channel-months (updated 16 Sep 2026): Shorts RPM is about $0.07-0.20 per 1,000 views in most niches, 3-14% of long-form RPM. Matching 1,000 long-form views takes 11,000-26,000 Shorts views in most niches. Shorts RPM by country: US $0.328, UK $0.166, CA $0.165. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- [THIRD-PARTY, 2026] Entertainment specifically (AIR): within the same channels, blended RPM was $4.58 in months with no Shorts vs $1.84 in Shorts months (-60%). Dose-response: $4.99 at 0 Shorts/month vs $1.15 at 21+ Shorts/month (-77%). For channels posting Shorts irregularly, Shorts made up 26% of views but 1.5% of revenue. AIR says causality is unproven. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- [THIRD-PARTY] AIR's conclusion: the economic value of Shorts lies in converting viewers to long-form, not in direct Shorts revenue. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- [OFFICIAL] Non-original Shorts (e.g. reuploads without meaningful edits) are ineligible for Shorts monetization. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)

### Inferences
- At ~$0.07-0.33 RPM, 1M Shorts views earns roughly $70-330. The same channel's long-form (entertainment RPM ~$4-5) earns that from about 15,000-70,000 long views. The 10M-Shorts-views YPP path is far harder for a new clip channel than 4,000 watch hours from long segments.
- The entertainment dose-response (21+ Shorts/month linked to much lower blended RPM) is a direct caution against "many more Shorts": the channel already posts about 30 a month.
- Clip channels should keep an eye on the "non-original" clause: Shorts cut from someone else's stream need commentary or editing value. Lolcow Cinema has the creators' permission (per project notes), but YouTube's reused-content review is separate.

### Gaps
- Whether YPP thresholds or the 45% split changed in 2026: the Help pages fetched show the values above, with no change found.
- No Shorts RPM figure specifically for commentary/clip channels (AIR's niche tables are images).
- Whether Shorts over 60 s (up to 3 min) earn differently was not found.
