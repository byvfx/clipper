# The Udder Truth: the first throw (#58)

**Build version: v1b** (user, 2026-10-23), where Rupert gets cut off. This is the script for
the storyboard in `../storyboards/throw_v1/`. It reworks the test take so it has teeth. The
coffee story and its correspondent stay; the target is now the whole pay-to-watch economy, the
show included.

Brass Eye devices used (see `../brass_eye_formats.md`):
- invented street jargon
- false-precision statistics
- the satellite-delay cross, cut short
- the anchor stealing her reporter's punchline
- the show condemning the thing it does: the last line is a donation ask

Rules check: no real person is named or quoted. "Coffee Brand Coffee" and Rupert are ours. No
"hosts".

**Build audio:** `throw_v1b_mix.mp3` (90.9 s). The working copy is in `../voice/` (local), and a
copy is on the NAS under `\\192.168.0.155\docker\clipper\udder_truth\audio\throw_v1\`. It is
Mother Moo's read and Rupert's read together; this is the playback to perform to.

---

**1. Logo sting.** No voice. Chrome title, hard stop.

**2. Desk, wide.** She looks down at her notes, then up into the lens, and holds.

> MOTHER MOO: Good evening. I'm Mother Moo. This is The Udder Truth.

**3. Slow push-in.** Grave. Cut to the graphic card: a coffee cup under a spotlight.

> MOTHER MOO: Tonight: coffee. For centuries, a humble breakfast drink. Now, experts warn, it has
> become something far darker.
> *[pause]* A business model.

**4. A graphic types on beside her, then goes full frame.** She speaks with rising alarm.

> **CARD:** WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES. Platform 50%. The man 30%. His lawyer, by March 20%.

> MOTHER MOO: Across the internet, grown men are asking you for money. Not for food. Not for
> rent. For "the experience". On the street, it's known as "feeding the cow".
> *[arch]* A phrase I find personally hurtful.
> *[urgent]* One in three viewers has now paid to watch a man eat. The other two are lying. Is
> your family safe? Is your wallet safe? Is your father, at this moment, in the garage, at three
> in the morning, typing the letter W?

**5. Snap back to calm; she turns to the monitor.** Cut to the location.

> MOTHER MOO: We go now, live, to our correspondent Rupert, who has stood outside a coffee shop,
> in the rain, since Tuesday, to establish whether it stocks Coffee Brand Coffee. Rupert.

**6. Rupert in the rain.** Satellite delay: he stares into the lens for 2.5 s, then starts, and
gets cut off mid-word.

> RUPERT: Yes, Mother Moo. I can confirm that after four days of exten—
> MOTHER MOO *(over him)*: Thank you, Rupert.

**7. Cutaway to the barista through the window.** This covers the splice in the take.

> MOTHER MOO *(voice over)*: *[pause]* It does not.
> *[pause]* Rupert has been out there four days. He has set up a GoFundMe.

**8. Back to the desk.** The sign-off.

> MOTHER MOO: That's all for tonight. The Udder Truth is funded entirely by viewers like you.
> *[flat]* Give generously.
> *[mischief]* Pour yourself something brown, darlings. *[dry]* And stay messy.
