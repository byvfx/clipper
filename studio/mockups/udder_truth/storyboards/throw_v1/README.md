## Panels

1. Logo sting: black, then the chrome title.
2. Desk, wide: she looks down, then up into the lens, and holds.
3. Slow push-in, then a cutaway to a graphic card (a coffee cup on the monitor).
4. A graphic types on beside her, then goes full frame.
5. She snaps back to calm and turns to the monitor; cut to the location.
6. The reporter in the rain under an umbrella: a satellite delay, he stares into the lens, then speaks.
7. A cutaway (a barista at the café window) covers the splice in the take.
8. Back to the desk for her answer and the sign-off.

Panel 7 is the "cutaway at every splice" rule from the WAN 3 Prime 30 s test (HANDOFF).
