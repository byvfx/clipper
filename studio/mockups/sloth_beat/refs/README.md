Two more 'Teen covers were shown in chat on 2026-11-14 but did not arrive as files (re-attach to
store them). Notes from the screen:
- **'Teen, August 1986** ("Back to school in style"): purple/lavender ground with a pink
  coverline column on the left; photo of two models top-right bleeding to the edge; yellow and
  cyan colour blocks behind "BOYS! SEX! PROBLEMS! ANSWERS!"; a small inset photo bottom-left with
  "FOIL THE SHYNESS TRAP"; a cyan "INSIDER'S REPORT" tab over a bottom-right photo. Coverlines in
  condensed sans, white or black, with round pink bullets.
- **'Teen, February 1987** ("Pull things together!"): pink masthead block with cyan 'TEEN, big
  diagonal pink/yellow/lavender panels, main photo right, two framed photos left (one on a
  diagonal pink panel), vertical "STAR GAZING" coverline, a yellow "GO TOGETHERS" slab, bullet
  lists; white and pink boxes for the lower coverlines.

Common 'Teen rules (1986-1989): one dominant studio photo on the right, two to four framed
secondary photos on the left/bottom, three or four flat panel colours per issue, no gradients,
no outlines on text, coverlines as short stacked lines with a bullet-dot sub-line, price/date in
tiny caps under the masthead, and the barcode or mailing label bottom-left.
