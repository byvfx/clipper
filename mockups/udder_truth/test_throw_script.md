# The Udder Truth: test throw, "The Fundraiser" (about 90 s)

The first full throw for #58: logo, Mother Moo at the desk, a throw to a correspondent on location,
back to the desk, sign-off. Tests the voice, both kinds of character animation, plates, graphics,
sound and the Resolve handoff in one piece.

- **Mother Moo's lines are verbatim from `test_take_script.md`**, which is already performed (Harriet's
  read C, the 51.5 s first clip). The only change is where we cut away: after "Coffee Brand Coffee"
  we go to the correspondent, and "It does not." becomes her answer when we come back. So the desk
  needs no new take.
- **New performance:** Rupert Churn (about 40 s), plus his rain and wind. Characters are in `cast.md`.
- **Splices:** WAN 3 Prime renders at most 15 s per piece and the head jumps at each join, so every
  long stretch has a planned cutaway (marked **SPLICE**). Line the desk's cutaways up with the first
  clip's existing joins (15.0, 28.6, 42.0 s).
- **Sound:** cue numbers (#) are from `test_sound_list.md`. No model audio anywhere.
- **Satire rules:** no real person is named or quoted; Rupert, the café and the townspeople are invented.
- **Runway frame:** what to generate for the storyboard (and later as start frames). Character and
  location tags (`@Moo`, `@Rupert`, `@Desk`, `@Cafe`) are the reference images from `cast.md`.
  Leave signage and on-screen words out of generated frames; text goes on in Fusion.

---

### 1. LOGO STING (0:00-0:05)
**Video:** placeholder for THE UDDER TRUTH sting (#59). Until it exists: black, then the title in plain chrome.
**Audio:** news intro sting (#2) with a faint moo/cowbell (#11) in the tail.
**Runway frame:** none (built in Houdini/Fusion).

### 2. DESK, WIDE (0:05-0:13)
**Video:** the wide desk framing from the hero image. Mother Moo looks down at her notes, then up into the lens. Holds.
**Audio:** studio room tone (#1) starts here and never stops on desk shots.

> **MOTHER MOO:** Good evening. I'm Mother Moo... and this... is **The Udder Truth.**

**Audio:** impact + shimmer (#3) on "Truth".
**Runway frame:** `@Moo seated at @Desk, wide shot, looking down at papers, 1990s British current-affairs studio, chrome and blue light, 16:9, Betacam video look.`

### 3. DESK, MEDIUM CLOSE-UP (0:13-0:24)
**Video:** slow push in. The monitor behind her shows a cup of coffee in harsh light. **SPLICE** cover: at "A *fundraiser*", cut to a full-frame graphic card, FUNDRAISER, chrome letters, red alarm stripe (Fusion).

> **MOTHER MOO:** *(leaning in, grave)* Tonight: coffee. For centuries, a humble breakfast drink. But now, experts warn, it may be something far darker.
> *(pause, one eyebrow up)* A *fundraiser.*

**Audio:** news bed (#4) in under "Tonight", ducked; riser into a stab (#5) on the graphic.
**Runway frame:** `@Moo at @Desk, medium close-up, leaning toward camera, grave expression, monitor behind her showing a single coffee cup under a spotlight, 1990s British news studio, 16:9.`

### 4. GRAPHIC OVER DESK (0:24-0:35)
**Video:** Mother Moo in medium shot. Beside her, a box graphic, FUNDRAISERS: THE FACTS, types on line by line: *They ask for money. They have a webcam. They offer "the experience."* **SPLICE** cover: the graphic goes full frame on "Is your family safe?".

> **MOTHER MOO:** *(fast, rising alarm)* Across the internet, men with webcams are asking you, the viewer, for money. For tickets. For seats. For... *"the experience."* Is your family safe? Is your *wallet* safe?

**Audio:** rising tension (#6) under it; computer bleeps (#21) as each line types on.
**Runway frame:** `@Moo at @Desk, medium shot framed left to leave room for a graphic on the right, alarmed expression, hands raised to chest height, 16:9.`

### 5. DESK, TURN TO MONITOR (0:35-0:47)
**Video:** she snaps back to calm and turns to her right, to a monitor showing the rainy café with a LIVE bug. **SPLICE** cover: on "Coffee Brand Coffee" cut straight to Rupert.

> **MOTHER MOO:** *(calm, turning to the monitor)* We go live now to our correspondent, who has been standing outside a coffee shop... in the rain... since Tuesday... to find out whether it stocks *Coffee Brand Coffee.*

**Audio:** bed out; transition whoosh + satellite link beep (#7) on the cut.
**Runway frame:** `@Moo at @Desk, three-quarter profile, turned toward a studio monitor that shows a rainy night street outside a café, 1990s news studio, 16:9.`

### 6. LOCATION, RUPERT, MEDIUM SHOT (0:47-1:02)
**Video:** night, heavy rain, outside the café. Rupert under a tartan umbrella with a broken spoke, foam mic, harsh on-camera light. Lower third: RUPERT CHURN, Our Man Outside A Coffee Shop. **Satellite delay:** for 2 s he stares into the lens, finger to earpiece, nodding, before he speaks.
Every location shot carries the field sound: rain on the umbrella (#12), the rainy street (#13), a wet car pass (#14), wind buffets on the mic (#16). His voice is thinner, like a hand mic.

> **RUPERT:** *(after the delay)* Thank you, Mother. Yes. I'm outside the Spilt Bean, where for four days now one question has hung over this community like... *(glances up at the rain)* ...weather. Does it stock Coffee Brand Coffee?

**Runway frame:** `@Rupert standing outside @Cafe at night in heavy rain, medium shot, tartan umbrella with one broken spoke, foam microphone, finger pressed to his earpiece, staring into the lens under a harsh on-camera light, 1990s British TV news, 16:9.`

### 7. CUTAWAY, CAFÉ WINDOW (1:02-1:08)  **SPLICE**
**Video:** through the steamed-up window: a barista behind the counter looks out at us, unimpressed, wipes a cup. Covers the join in Rupert's take.

> **RUPERT** *(voice over)*: The staff will neither confirm nor deny. They have, however, asked me to stop breathing on the glass.

**Audio:** the street bed continues; muffled café chatter (#15) through the glass.
**Runway frame:** `View through a steamed-up café window at night, rain on the glass, a bored barista behind the counter staring out at the camera while drying a cup, warm interior light, 1990s British TV news, 16:9. No readable signage.`

### 8. LOCATION, RUPERT, CLOSE-UP (1:08-1:24)
**Video:** tighter on Rupert. Mid-line the café door opens beside him (door bell) and he shuffles aside. Then he presses the earpiece and listens; we don't hear her question.

> **RUPERT:** Locals are frightened. One woman told me, quote, "Can you move? You're in the doorway." *(door bell, he steps aside)* She would not be drawn on the fundraiser.
> *(finger to earpiece, listening)* Sorry, Mother, you're breaking up... *(static)* ...have I given? I... Yes. Forty pounds. For "the experience." *(rain, a long beat)* It hasn't arrived.

**Audio:** door + bell (#15), distant siren (#17), static and line dropout (#19) on "breaking up".
**Runway frame:** `@Rupert close-up outside @Cafe at night, rain dripping off his glasses, finger pressed to his earpiece, wounded but dignified expression, harsh on-camera light, 1990s British TV news, 16:9.`

### 9. INSERT, THE UMBRELLA (1:24-1:27)
**Video:** a gust turns his umbrella inside out. No dialogue, so this one can be plain image-to-video from shot 8's frame (a test of Runway's own video).

> **RUPERT:** *(over the wind, not giving up)* Back to the studio.

**Audio:** big wind gust (#16), a thunder clap (#8) as we cut.
**Runway frame:** `@Rupert outside @Cafe at night as a gust of wind blows his tartan umbrella inside out, rain sheeting sideways, 1990s British TV news, 16:9.`

### 10. DESK, TO LENS (1:27-1:33)
**Video:** Mother Moo turns back from the monitor to the lens. Says it. Then the long deadpan hold, 3 s, tiny smile at the very end.

> **MOTHER MOO:** It does not.

**Audio:** the thunder rings out and everything drops to room tone (#1) for the hold. The silence is the joke.
**Runway frame:** `@Moo at @Desk, medium close-up, facing the lens, completely deadpan, 1990s news studio, 16:9.`

### 11. DESK, SIGN-OFF (1:33-1:40)
**Video:** back to the wide. Big, warm, theatrical. Wink, hold 2 s.

> **MOTHER MOO:** That's all for tonight. Pour yourself something brown, darlings... and stay messy.

**Audio:** news outro (#9) with the moo/cowbell (#11), twinkle (#10) on the wink.
**Runway frame:** `@Moo at @Desk, wide shot, arms open, warm theatrical smile, mid-wink, 1990s news studio, 16:9.`
