# The Udder Truth: sounds for the test throw

For the bake-off (#58): one rough mix laid under every model's desk → reporter → desk throw.
Search terms are for Artlist (or any library). Grab two or three options for each and choose
in the edit. WAV, 48 kHz. Keep the files out of git (put them in `sounds/` here, which is
gitignored, or on the NAS).

## Desk (Mother Moo), in script order

| # | Sound | Where it goes | Search |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Studio room tone, 1-2 min | under every desk line and pause, so it never goes silent | `room tone studio`, `broadcast studio ambience` |

  reporter answers), not library sounds.
