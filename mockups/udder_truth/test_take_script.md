# The Udder Truth: Mother Moo test take (about 60 s)

For the model bake-off (#58). One phone take, landscape, head and shoulders, looking into the
lens. Do it two or three times; we use the best one for every model. Stage directions in
[brackets] are for you, not to be read. Each beat tests something the models struggle with
(noted on the right).

---

**[Look down at "notes", then up into the lens, slowly. Hold 2 seconds, no expression.]**
*(a long still hold: identity and blinking)*

Good evening. I'm Mother Moo... and this... is **The Udder Truth.**
*(big open vowels: "Moo", "Udder", "Truth")*

**[Lean in slightly. Gravely.]**
Tonight: coffee. For centuries, a humble breakfast drink. But now, experts warn, it may be
something far darker.
*(steady talking, hard consonants: "p", "b", "k")*

**[Pause. One eyebrow up.]**
A *fundraiser.*
*(a single small expression change)*

**[Fast, rising alarm, hands may come up to chest height but never over your mouth.]**
Across the internet, men with webcams are asking you, the viewer, for money. For tickets.
For seats. For... *"the experience."* Is your family safe? Is your *wallet* safe?
*(fast speech, emotion shift, hands in frame)*

**[Snap back to calm. Turn your head a little to your right, as if to a monitor.]**
We go live now to our correspondent, standing outside a coffee shop... in the rain...
for reasons we have not been told.
*(head turn: profile angle, then back)*

**[Turn back to the lens. Long deadpan hold, 3 seconds. Tiny smile at the very end.]**
*(the hardest one: stillness with micro-expression)*

**[Big, warm, theatrical.]**
That's all for tonight. Pour yourself something brown, darlings... and stay messy.
**[Wink. Hold 2 seconds.]**
*(wink and a closed-eye moment)*

---

Notes:
- Don't try to sound like her; the voice changer does that. Do give it her attitude: grand,
  grave, catty.
- About 130 words, so roughly 60 s with the holds. Running long is fine.
- Recording tips: rear camera if you can, steady phone, focus/exposure locked on your face,
  HDR video off, no Cinematic/Portrait mode, soft light from the front, quiet room.
