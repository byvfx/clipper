# Weekly article example (the owner's own copy, issue 02, 2026-10-24)

The voice the weekly's articles should have: a real feature, not a list of bullet points.
Context first, then the evidence trail, the receipts from the archive, the reactions, and the
rumor saved for the last line. Long sentences with em-dashes, plain verbs, a little relish.
This is the reference for a future writer prompt (HANDOFF "To do next" 8). Verbatim quotes
still come only from the stream's quote pool.

**Headline:** THE 'FAMILY / EMERGENCY'

**Deck:** From a “family emergency” cover story to a 2:30 a.m. affair confession, the H3 empire is in freefall—and rumor has it Tamara is pregnant.

**Byline:** BY THE LOLCOW DESK

**Left column:**

The official line was an “indefinite hiatus” for a “family emergency.” That cover lasted until 2:30 a.m., when Hila dropped a bombshell alleging lies and a six-month affair—which Ethan reposted himself. Behind the scenes, the crew is scrambling. Claiming they only found out on Wednesday, AB is publicly sweating rent with a baby on the way, while Harley is lobbying to keep the show alive. By Friday, Ethan was begging for privacy and surrendering his equity in H3 and Teddy Fresh.

Meanwhile, the internet had already identified the alleged other woman as H3 staffer Tamara, piecing together a trail of crew unfollows, joint Thing 1 and Thing 2 costumes, 4:00 a.m. Subnautica streams, and footage of her sending Ethan shopping requests. According to Kino Casino’s back-of-the-napkin math, the divorce could cost Ethan 60 percent or more of the empire.

**Right column:**

In the aftermath, the archive has become brutal viewing: Ethan swearing on Fresh and Fit that he’d never want anyone else, his Hila thigh tattoo, Trisha claiming he secretly craved the rich bachelor life, and his own “do we need to fake a divorce?” gag. While devastated fans call out sick—one calling the split more painful than their own actual divorce—Ethan’s detractors are celebrating.
