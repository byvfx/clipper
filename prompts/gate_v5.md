## has_clip
Following the editor's brief above, this window holds at least one moment that the viewer
described there would understand and enjoy as a Short without having watched the rest of
the stream: a payoff (a joke, a fail, a reveal, a win), a strong opinion stated cleanly, a
surprising result, or a genuine reaction from the show's own people. Setup with no payoff,
or a moment that only works with chat visible, does not count.
## third_party_audio
The brief's rule on other creators' video or stream audio rejects this window's best
moment.
## casino_gameplay
The window's best moment is about gambling play (slots, keno, roulette, blackjack, poker
hands, sports bets, spins, wins or losses on a casino or betting site) or promotes a
gambling site. A story that only mentions a casino in passing does not count.
## needs_context
The viewer described in the brief would be lost without something set up earlier in this
same stream, or could misread the moment (including making the streamer look worse than
the full context shows).
## score
How well the window's best moment would do as a Short with this viewer. Be harsh: most
windows are a 1 or 2.
- 1: nothing worth posting
- 2: a flicker of something, no real payoff
- 3: a weak moment most viewers would scroll past
- 4: an okay moment, not worth posting
- 5: decent but forgettable
- 6: good, would post on a slow day
- 7: strong, would post without hesitation
- 8: very strong, likely one of the stream's best
- 9: excellent, the stream's best moment
- 10: exceptional, could spread well beyond the show's fans
## category
What kind of moment the window's best moment is.
- fail: someone fails, embarrasses themselves or gets caught out
- win: someone wins, succeeds or gets a satisfying result
- joke: a joke, a bit or a roast lands
- insight: a clear point, a story or new information worth hearing
- reaction: a genuine reaction to something shown or said
- hot_take: a strong, opinionated take stated cleanly
