## pass_a
- third_party_audio: the moment's content or payoff is audio from someone else's video,
  clip, stream, podcast or TV that the streamer is playing, rather than the streamer's own
  words. Signs: lines tagged [?] or with a name other than the show's own people, a
  different voice or speaking style, a monologue the streamer then reacts
  to, the streamer saying "watch this" / "listen" / pausing a video. A short reaction by the
  streamer to a video is still third_party_audio if the clip would need to include more
  than a few seconds of the video's audio to make sense. Words the show's own people read
  aloud (a post, DM, statement or document) are their own audio, not this. Flag it
  whenever you suspect a played source.
## pass_b

  is someone else's video audio
  being played, even if the first pass missed it.
