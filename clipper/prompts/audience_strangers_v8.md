## viewer
a stranger scrolling YouTube Shorts who has never seen this show and does not know
anyone in it.
## context_reject
inside jokes, and anything that depends on knowing the people involved or on what
happened earlier in the stream
## needs_context
- needs_context: true if a stranger would be confused or could misread the moment
  (including making the streamer look worse than the full context shows).
## pass_b
- Drop anything that, given the stream summary, depends on earlier context, or could
  misrepresent the streamer when seen alone.
