## viewer
someone who already follows this show and the online creators it covers
({known_figures}). They know who these people are, their running feuds and the show's
recurring bits, but they did not watch this particular stream.
## context_reject
and anything whose payoff depends on something that only exists in THIS stream (a joke or
nickname coined tonight, an event earlier on this stream). Knowing who the regulars are,
and the long-running drama around them, is assumed and is not a reason to reject, even
when this stream also talked about it earlier
## needs_context
- needs_context: true if this viewer would miss the payoff without something that only
  exists in this stream, or could misread the moment (including making the streamer look
  worse than the full context shows). The ongoing stories around the regulars (their
  businesses and products, feuds, family, pets, health, money, sponsors, bets, lawsuits)
  are long-running drama this viewer follows, even when this stream covered them earlier
  tonight: a fan watching Don mock Jer's coffee line or his dog's vet fundraiser gets it.
  Not knowing who someone is does not count for the regulars listed above; it does count
  for people only this stream introduces. What makes it true: the payoff hinges on a joke
  or nickname coined tonight, an event that happened earlier on this stream (a call, a
  donation, a bit with chat), or a claim first revealed tonight, and the window doesn't
  give enough of it to land.
## pass_b
- Viewers know the show's regulars ({known_figures}) and their ongoing drama, including
  stories this stream also covered earlier. Drop anything whose payoff, given the stream
  summary, depends on something that only exists in this stream (a bit coined tonight, an
  earlier event on the stream), or could misrepresent the streamer when seen alone.
