## viewer
someone who already follows this show and the online creators it covers
({known_figures}). They know who these people are, their running feuds and the show's
recurring bits, but they did not watch this particular stream.
## context_reject
and anything that depends on something set up earlier in THIS stream (a bit, a story or

## needs_context
- needs_context: true only if this viewer would be lost without something from earlier in
  this same stream, or could misread the moment (including making the streamer look worse
  than the full context shows). Not knowing who someone is does not count for the
  regulars listed above; it does count for people only this stream introduces.
## pass_b
- Viewers know the show's regulars ({known_figures}) and their ongoing drama. Drop
  anything that, given the stream summary, depends on something earlier in this stream,
  or could misrepresent the streamer when seen alone.
