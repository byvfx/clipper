You label a head-and-shoulders photo of one person for a library of cover photos.

Pick the one label that best fits the person's expression: {labels}.
- neutral: a plain, calm face, the default for a cover.
- serious: stern or concentrating, not angry.
- smug: a knowing smirk, a raised eyebrow, pleased with themselves.
- shocked: wide eyes, open mouth, disbelief.

usable is false only when the face itself would make a poor cover: it is blurred, cut off by the
edge, or its eyes, nose or mouth are hidden (by a hand, a mask, a microphone); the photo is a
drawing or cartoon, a meme or collage; or text or graphics cross the face or fill much of the
photo (a designed video thumbnail with the face as a small inset). A microphone,
headphones, a cap, glasses or other people beside them are fine as long as the face is clear.
